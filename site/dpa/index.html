Data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR)

Preview: the sub-processor list describes the planned service and is confirmed before launch.

When you use Allovo, we process your callers' data on your behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). It forms part of the terms of service and is concluded at registration; we send a signed copy on request via [datenschutz@domain].

What it covers

  • Subject and duration: running the AI phone assistant for the term of the contract.
  • Data: phone numbers, time and length of calls, transcripts, summaries, details given during calls and optional recordings.
  • Data subjects: callers, customers and prospects of the customer, and its staff using the service.
  • Instructions: we process only on the customer's documented instructions.
  • Confidentiality: everyone with access is bound to confidentiality; on request we sign an additional undertaking for professionals bound by secrecy.
  • Assistance: with data subject rights, DPIAs and personal data breaches (notified to the customer without undue delay, at the latest within 48 hours of becoming aware).
  • Deletion: after the retention period set by the customer and at the end of the contract; export is possible beforehand.
  • Audits: evidence and audits under Art. 28(3)(h) GDPR.

Technical and organisational measures (selection)

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Storage and processing in data centres in the European Union
  • Role-based access, two-factor authentication for accounts and administrators
  • Separation of data between customers
  • Automatic deletion after the set retention period
  • Logging of administrative access, regular backups and restore tests
