Pricing questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

With Allovo an AI receptionist costs €0.15 per minute of calls, with everything included. A small business with 300 minutes of calls a month pays €45, and a busy clinic with 1,500 minutes pays €225.

Is there a monthly fee or a setup fee?

No. You pay only for the minutes your calls use. There is no monthly fee, no setup fee and no minimum.

Do complex calls or long instructions cost more?

No. Every minute costs €0.15, however detailed your instructions are and whatever happens during the call.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You start with 60 free minutes to test your agent on your own calls. No card is needed.

Can I limit how much I spend?

Yes. Set a monthly spending limit and you get an alert when you reach 80% of it.

What does an AI answering service cost?

The same €0.15 per minute. If your AI answering service only takes evening and weekend calls, you pay only for those minutes.
