AI answering service questions

What is an AI answering service?

An AI answering service answers your business calls when you or your team can’t. It talks with callers in a natural voice, answers common questions, takes detailed messages, books appointments and sends you each message as soon as the call ends.

How much does an AI answering service cost?

With Allovo you pay €0.15 per minute of calls, with everything included and no monthly plan. A business that sends it 200 minutes of calls a month pays €30.

How is it different from an AI receptionist?

It is the same agent with a different job. An answering service covers the calls you can’t take and makes sure you get the message. An AI receptionist answers every call first and runs your phone like a front desk.

Can it answer only after hours?

Yes. Forward your calls only in the evenings and at weekends, or only when nobody picks up. You choose which calls reach it and can change it any time.

What happens with urgent calls?

You tell it what counts as urgent for your business. Urgent messages are marked at the top and emailed straight away, so the right person can call back.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays the same. You forward calls to your answering service, or get a new local number in a few clicks.

Which languages does it speak?

It answers in your caller’s language, including English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Romanian and Bulgarian.
