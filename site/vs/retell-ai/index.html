Retell AI pricing questions

How much does Retell AI cost per minute?

Retell AI lists $0.07 to $0.31 a minute. That is $0.055 for the voice platform plus the voice and AI model you choose. Common setups land between about $0.12 and $0.18 a minute before add-ons such as a knowledge base.

Does Retell AI have a monthly fee?

Retell AI is pay as you go, billed per second. Phone numbers cost $2 a month, and concurrent calls above 20 cost $8 per line a month.

Is Allovo cheaper than Retell AI?

It depends on the setup. Allovo costs €0.15 a minute with voice, AI model and add-ons included, so the price stays the same whichever model or voice your calls need.

Does Retell AI host data in the EU?

Retell AI’s compliance page says it does not currently operate services within the European Union. Allovo keeps call data in Europe.

Can I move my Retell AI agents to Allovo?

Yes. Copy your prompt and knowledge into a new agent, test it with your usual calls, then forward your number or move your SIP trunk.
