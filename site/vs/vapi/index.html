Vapi pricing questions

How much does Vapi cost per minute?

Vapi charges $0.05 a minute for hosting, plus speech, AI model and voice at the providers’ cost. Vapi’s own calculator puts 1,000 minutes at about $82 to $129, which is $0.08 to $0.13 a minute.

Does Vapi have a monthly fee?

The usage-only plan has no monthly fee but allows four calls at once. The Core plan is $29 a month for ten calls at once, and extra lines cost $10 a month each.

Is Allovo cheaper than Vapi?

For small volumes on the Core plan they are close. Allovo keeps one price of €0.15 a minute with everything included, and adds a dashboard, client accounts and white label without extra fees.

Does Vapi host data in the EU?

Vapi lists an EU region on its custom Premier plan only. Allovo keeps call data in Europe on every plan.

Can I move my Vapi agents to Allovo?

Yes. Copy your prompt and knowledge into a new agent, test it with your usual calls, then forward your number or move your SIP trunk.
