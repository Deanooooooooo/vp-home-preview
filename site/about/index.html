About Allovo

Allovo is built by the team behind Vdiga, an AI phone receptionist that answers calls for businesses in Bulgaria. The company behind both is „Raboti“ DPK, based in Sofia, in the European Union.

What we build

Allovo answers business phone calls with AI: it talks to callers, books appointments, takes messages and sends a summary after every call. Businesses use it directly, agencies sell it under their own brand, and developers build on it through the API.

We started with Vdiga, our AI receptionist for businesses in Bulgaria. Allovo brings the same technology to more languages and markets.

How we work

One price per minute

No subscription, no set-up fee, no minimum term. The bill is the minutes you use.

Data in the EU

Call recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored in the European Union, with a data processing agreement.

AI says it is AI

Every assistant introduces itself as an AI assistant, as the EU AI Act requires. The notice cannot be switched off.

Business customers only

We work with companies and professionals, not consumers, and we write our terms for that.
