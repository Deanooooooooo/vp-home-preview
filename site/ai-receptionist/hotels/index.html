Hotel AI receptionist questions

What does an AI receptionist do for a hotel?

It answers calls in the guest’s language at any hour, answers questions about rooms, prices, policies and the area, and sends room requests, late arrivals and messages to your reception by email.

Which languages does it speak?

It answers in the caller’s language, including English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Romanian and Bulgarian, and writes to your reception in your language.

How much does it cost for a hotel?

€0.15 per minute of calls with everything included. A hotel with 3,000 minutes of calls a month pays €450.

Can it check availability in my booking system?

It sends room requests to your reception by email out of the box. Tell us which booking system you use and we will show you how a connection would work.

Will guests know they are talking to AI?

Yes. Your receptionist introduces itself as the hotel’s AI assistant in the greeting, as EU rules require.
