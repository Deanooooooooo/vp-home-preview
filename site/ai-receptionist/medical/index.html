AI medical receptionist questions

What is an AI medical receptionist?

An AI medical receptionist answers calls to a clinic or practice in a natural voice. It books appointments, takes prescription and test-result requests, answers practice questions and passes urgent calls on according to the practice’s rules.

Who answers medical questions?

Your clinical team. The AI receptionist handles bookings, requests and practice information, sends medical questions to your team as a message and tells callers with an emergency to ring 112.

How much does an AI medical receptionist cost?

€0.15 per minute of calls with everything included. A practice with 1,500 minutes of calls a month pays €225.

Where is patient data stored?

Calls, transcripts and recordings are stored in Europe. You set how long they are kept and can sign a data processing agreement with us.

Can it book with a specific doctor?

Yes. Connect each doctor’s calendar and set which appointment types each doctor offers.
