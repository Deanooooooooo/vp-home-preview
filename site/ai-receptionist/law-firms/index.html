Law firm AI receptionist questions

What does an AI receptionist do for a law firm?

It answers calls, asks your intake questions for new enquiries, books first consultations with the right lawyer, takes messages for existing clients and explains your fees and process from your own wording.

Who gives legal advice?

Your lawyers. The AI receptionist takes the details, books the consultation and sends the lawyer a clear intake note.

How much does it cost for a law firm?

€0.15 per minute of calls with everything included. A firm with 1,500 minutes of calls a month pays €225.

Is client information kept confidential?

Calls, transcripts and recordings are stored in Europe, access is limited by role, and you set how long they are kept.

Can it book with a specific lawyer?

Yes. Connect each lawyer’s calendar and set which matters and consultation types each one takes.
