Dental AI receptionist questions

What does a dental AI receptionist do?

It answers calls to your practice, books and moves appointments in your diary, answers questions about treatments, prices and opening hours, and sends urgent calls and messages to your team.

Can it handle dental emergencies?

It asks the triage questions you set, offers your emergency slots and marks the call as urgent for the dentist. For anything life-threatening it tells the caller to ring the emergency number.

How much does a dental AI receptionist cost?

€0.15 per minute of calls with everything included. A practice with 600 minutes of calls a month pays €90.

Does it work with my practice diary?

It books into a connected calendar. Tell us which practice software you use and we will show you how it connects.

Is patient data safe?

Calls and transcripts are stored in Europe, and you choose how long to keep them. You can sign a data processing agreement with us.
