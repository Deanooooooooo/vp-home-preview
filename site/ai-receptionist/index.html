AI receptionist questions

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers your business phone with a natural voice. It talks with callers, answers their questions, books appointments into your calendar and sends you a summary of each call.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

With Allovo you pay €0.15 per minute of calls, with everything included. There is no monthly fee and no setup fee, so a business that takes 600 minutes of calls a month pays €90.

Will callers know they are speaking to AI?

Yes. Your receptionist introduces itself as your AI assistant at the start of the call, as EU rules require, and then helps the caller in a natural voice.

Can it book appointments into my calendar?

Yes. Connect your calendar and it books, moves and cancels appointments during the call, then texts the caller a confirmation.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Forward your existing number to your AI receptionist, or get a new local number in a few clicks.

What happens when it can’t answer a question?

It takes the caller’s name, number and question and sends them to you straight away, so you can call back with the answer.

Which languages does it speak?

It answers in your caller’s language, including English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Romanian and Bulgarian.
