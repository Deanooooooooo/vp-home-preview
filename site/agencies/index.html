Build one strong agent

White label questions

What is white label voice AI?

White label voice AI lets an agency sell AI phone agents under its own brand. Clients see the agency’s name, domain and invoices, while the voice platform runs in the background.

What does white label cost with Allovo?

You pay €0.15 per minute of calls with everything included. There is no platform fee, no seat fee and no charge per client account.

Will my clients see the Allovo name?

No. The dashboard, emails, call summaries and invoices carry your brand on your own domain.

Can I set a different price for each client?

Yes. Set a price per minute or a monthly bundle for each client. We bill you at €0.15 a minute and you keep the difference.

We used another platform’s agency plan. Can we move?

Yes. Copy each agent’s instructions and knowledge into a client sub-account, test it, then forward the client’s number. Your clients keep their numbers.

Which languages can I sell in?

Your agents answer in the caller’s language, including English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Romanian and Bulgarian.
