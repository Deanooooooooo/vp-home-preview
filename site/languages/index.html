Language questions

Which languages do Allovo agents speak?

English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Romanian and Bulgarian, each with its own voice.

Can one agent answer in several languages?

Yes. Write your instructions once and the agent answers each caller in their language, or in the language you set for each phone number.

Does a call in another language cost more?

No. Every language costs the same €0.15 per minute with everything included.

Can I get local phone numbers in other countries?

Yes. Get a local number in each country you serve, or forward your existing numbers to your agent.
