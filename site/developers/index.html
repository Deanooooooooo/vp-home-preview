Developer questions

Is there a voice AI API?

Yes. The Allovo API lets you create agents, start calls, read transcripts and recordings, and receive call events by webhook. Everything in the dashboard is also in the API.

How much does the voice AI API cost?

€0.15 per minute of calls, with speech recognition, the AI model and the voice included. There is no monthly platform fee, and phone numbers are billed at cost.

Can I bring my own SIP trunk?

Yes. Connect your SIP trunk or carrier, or buy local numbers through the API.

Can the agent call my own API during a call?

Yes. Define tools with your endpoints, and the agent calls them during the conversation, for example to check free slots or look up an order.

Can I move agents from another voice platform?

Yes. Copy the prompt and tools into a new agent over the API or in the dashboard, then point your number or SIP trunk at it.
