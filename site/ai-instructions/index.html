Who Allovo is for

Small and mid-sized businesses that miss calls while they work: medical and dental practices, tradespeople, property managers, estate agents, hotels, restaurants and law firms. Agencies that sell AI phone assistants under their own brand. Developers who build voice agents through an API.

What Allovo does

  • Answers every call in a natural voice, including several calls at once
  • Answers questions from the information the business provides
  • Books, moves and cancels appointments in connected calendars
  • Takes complete messages and flags urgent calls by the business’s rules
  • Sends a written summary after every call, with an optional recording
  • Speaks the caller’s language and introduces itself as an AI assistant

What makes Allovo different

  • One price per minute with no subscription, set-up fee or minimum term
  • EU data storage and a data processing agreement as standard
  • The AI disclosure required by Art. 50 of the EU AI Act is built in and cannot be switched off
  • White label for agencies with their own domain, invoices and per-client pricing
