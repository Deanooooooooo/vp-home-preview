    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Can my business use Allovo directly, without an agency?

    Yes. Sign up, build your agent from a template, connect a phone number and go live. If you would rather have it set up for you, a partner agency can do it.

    What is a white-label voice AI platform?

    It is the engine behind AI phone agents that an agency sells under its own name. Allovo runs the calls, voices and language model; your clients only see your brand, your domain and your invoices.

    Which languages can my agents speak?

    The ten languages you can hear on this page: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Bulgarian, Romanian and Swedish. Each one uses a voice built for that language, not an English voice reading a translation.

    Where is call data processed and stored?

    Inside the European Union. Speech recognition, the language model, the voice and storage all run in EU regions, and the sub-processor list names every company and region.

    How much does Allovo cost?

    One price per minute with speech, language model, voice and platform included. Phone numbers are charged at cost. There is no monthly platform fee.

    Can I move agents from Retell or Vapi?

    Yes. Import an agent’s prompt, tools and settings, test it side by side, then point the phone number to Allovo when you are happy.

    How fast can a business go live?

    Most businesses go live the same day: pick a template, describe your business, connect your calendar and forward your number.

    Is there an API?

    Yes. Everything in the dashboard is in the REST API, with webhooks for every call event and SIP trunking for your own carrier.

    Will my clients see the Allovo name?

    No. The dashboard, login page, emails and invoices carry your name, logo, colours and domain.
